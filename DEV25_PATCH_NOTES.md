# v1.1.2 Dev25 — Unit unlock pricing and uncontrollable infantry recycling

- Reaver and HERC unit unlocks now cost 100 credits each (explicit unit-shop overrides).
- Infested Liberator unit unlock increased from 50 to 100 credits.
- Recycled Armor Suits now refunds 8 minerals and 2 gas when an eligible player-owned Terran infantry unit marked uncommandable by Slay dies, including Cloning Technology copies, Meat Grinder Marines, and Warfield Drop Pod troops. All controllable eligible infantry, even if Meat Grinder is owned, retain the normal 25-mineral / 10-gas refund.
- Shared per-unit uncommandable marker means the discount applies independently of boon ownership, without altering spawn AI or other death triggers.
- All Dev24 features retained; offline checks only, actual SC2 gameplay not yet verified.
